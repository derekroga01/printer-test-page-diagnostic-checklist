# Printer Diagnostic Checklist

Print one sheet at a time, at 100 percent or Actual Size, on plain paper. Tick each item as you check it, and print the sheet again after any fix.

## Quick sheet: general first check

- [ ] Printed the Quick sheet first
- [ ] Text is dark and readable
- [ ] No colour is missing
- [ ] No banding or lines
- [ ] Lines and text are straight and sharp

## Black and White sheet: faded or streaky prints

- [ ] Grey steps are smooth and even
- [ ] No pale patches or white streaks
- [ ] Ran one cleaning cycle if steps faded
- [ ] Checked black cartridge level
- [ ] Printed again on normal quality, plain paper

## CMYK sheet: one colour missing or wrong colours

- [ ] Cyan block is solid
- [ ] Magenta block is solid
- [ ] Yellow block is solid
- [ ] Black block is solid
- [ ] Reseated any missing cartridge and cleaned once
- [ ] Checked cartridge order and levels

## Color sheet: banding or lines across the page

- [ ] No horizontal bands or stripes in solid colour
- [ ] Ran a nozzle check and one cleaning cycle
- [ ] Ran print head alignment if bands stayed in place
- [ ] Noted whether bands affect one colour or every colour

## Alignment sheet: crooked, doubled, or blurry text

- [ ] Straight lines are straight
- [ ] Text is sharp, with no doubling or shadows
- [ ] Ran the alignment routine if lines lean
- [ ] Reloaded paper neatly, guides snug against the stack
- [ ] Printed one sheet at a time to check the feed

## Printing correctly

- [ ] Printed at 100 percent or Actual Size, no scaling
- [ ] Paper size matches the paper in the tray, A4 or US Letter
- [ ] Printed one sided, on plain, dry, flat paper

Done when the matching sheet prints clean, and the good print is kept as proof.
